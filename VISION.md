# Vocca: Vision

> Your voice should be able to type anywhere and talk back — privately, on your own machine,
> with nothing to hide and nothing sent away.

---

## The bet in one sentence

Wispr Flow proved that voice-to-text-everywhere is a daily habit worth paying for — but it's
closed, cloud, and rented. **Vocca is the open-source, local-first version of that habit**,
with an agent voice-loop (a smarter, open SKI) layered on top, and a path to a premium hosted
tier later. The value is the private, instant, extensible voice layer for your Mac.

---

## Why now

- **The daily habit is proven.** Dictation that types polished text into any app is sticky;
  people already pay monthly for it.
- **Local models are finally good enough.** On Apple Silicon, on-device ASR and TTS (Kokoro)
  run fast enough to make the whole loop offline and private.
- **Privacy is a real, unmet reason to switch.** Wispr sends your audio to the cloud; many
  users and orgs can't or won't. An open, local alternative is a genuine gap.
- **Voice + agents is the frontier.** SKI showed a local voice loop with an agent; almost no
  open tool combines great dictation *and* a voice agent that can *act*.

---

## The founder's edge

**Full-stack developer + ML engineer.** The edge is integration and UX — a tight local audio
pipeline, reliable system-wide text injection, a clean widget — plus the action/agent layer
that turns voice into *doing*, and the option to train the hosted models for the later tier.
No proprietary data or credentials required to ship the open core.

---

## What makes it defensible (for an open project)

The moat here is **polish + community + local-first extensibility**, not a research secret —
so this is a "genuinely useful, big-traction OSS" play. It compounds three ways: stars and
trust from a great free local tool; an action/MCP layer that ties into the founder's agent
work; and a later open-core upsell (hosted, trained models) that never has to cripple the
free experience.

---

## The shape of the product

- **Dictation-first:** hotkey → talk → polished text in any app. The daily hook.
- **Then a voice agent:** it talks back through local Kokoro TTS, takes turns, and — a step
  past SKI — is context-aware and can act (run commands, drive MCP tools and coding agents).
- **Always local and private in the open core.** The premium cloud tier (our own trained
  models, Wispr-style) comes later, opt-in, and separate.

## Scope, held deliberately

macOS only for now. Local Kokoro TTS now. Fully open-source core. The hosted premium tier is
a future chapter we design seams for — not something we build today, and never a reason to
weaken the local experience.

---

## Non-goals

- Cross-platform before macOS is genuinely good.
- Cloud in the open core, or any audio leaving the device by default.
- A crippled free tier to upsell the hosted models.
- An over-built assistant before dictation is excellent.

---

## The one-line mantra

**Speak; it appears, or it answers — instantly, on your machine, and yours to change.**
