## Design decisions (made here, justified, recorded)

**D1 — The driver lives in a new VoccaBootstrap file, composed by a named recipe, not an
extension inside `configure`.** `Sources/VoccaBootstrap/ConverseLoopDriver.swift` (the driver)
+ `Sources/VoccaBootstrap/ConverseWiring.swift` (the recipe `extension AppBootstrap` +
`RefusingContinuousCapture`). Justification: (a) the probe contract — `configure` may not
provision models, may not block, may not start capture (`AppBootstrap.swift:246-250,63-66`; the
`kokoroSynthesizer` construction-only warrant `:904-909`); the driver takes *recipes* (lazy
closures) for the engine, the cleanup provider and the synthesizer, so construction is pure and
the probe stays green; (b) the G5 pin — `AppBootstrap.swift`'s diff is then three root slots +
the recipe call, reviewable in one screen; (c) testability — the driver is constructible over
fakes from `HarnessTests` **and** from the probe (the `SpeechDrive` precedent). The recipe
pattern is `assembleShippingLadder`'s (`:812-832`).

**D2 — The capture stream is driven by the driver's own main-actor drive task, the
`ComposedTurnDriver` shape generalized.** `start()` opens the capture (`capture.start()` —
`StreamingCapture`'s ownership refusal backs the machine's at-most-one-capture rule) and spawns
one drive task: `for await frame in stream { loop.feed(frame); applyPendingEffects() }`. The
loop is synchronous and owner-isolated; the owner is this task, on the main actor (the capture
tick is main-actor-asserted, `StreamingCapture.tick` `:238-243`; the loop is non-Sendable —
confinement to one task, never `@unchecked`). The utterance pipeline (ASR → cleanup → reply →
`scheduleReply`) runs in a `@MainActor` child task so **the feed never pauses** — capture is
continuous and never stopped at interrupt time (the C10 continuity doctrine); the loop's own
guards (`scheduleReply` refused in `.uttering`/`.playing`) make the late-landing reply safe —
never speak over the user. `stop()` cancels the child task, stops the capture, stops the loop.

**D3 — ASR resolves through the current resolver, once per committed utterance.** The driver
takes `asrProvider: @Sendable () -> (any ASREngine)?`; the composition passes
`{ resolver.engineIfReady() }` — the existing resolve-once engine, read at the moment the
utterance commits. Mid-session swap semantics: a settings switch mints a new resolver through the
one factory (`AppBootstrap.swift:255-258`); the provider reads the *current* resolver, so a swap
between turns uses the new engine, and a swap mid-turn cannot — the engine is resolved once per
utterance, never captured across turns (the egress fold's resolve-once doctrine, `:549-554`). An
unprepared engine answers nil → the honest `.asrFailed` drop (the readiness-gate semantics; the
model missing is a notice, never a hang).

**D4 — `onStateChange` is delivered, not re-typed.** The driver's `onStateChange` parameter is
passed straight to the loop's N1 hook (frozen at `TurnTakingLoop.swift:107-108,132`) and the
composition wires it into the root's new `converseStateSink` slot. **The widget-converse aspect
owns all state vocabulary and rendering** (`prd.md:274`); this aspect ships no speculative
surface — the deliverable is the hook delivered (slot + ordering pin in tests). The honest
failure notice's event is the typed `ConverseTurnFailure` (VoccaCore — the only shared home;
VoccaUI may not import VoccaBootstrap, `ModuleBoundaryTests.swift:225-245`), delivered through
the root's `converseFailureSink` slot; widget-converse renders both slots. In this aspect both
slots default to recording closures (the honest, headless-testable default), replaced by
widget-converse.

**D5 — Capture failure is the honest stop.** If the capture stream ends while the driver still
believes the session is running (abnormal end — the converter-error path
`StreamingCapture.swift:249-257` finishes the stream after releasing the device), the driver
calls `loop.reportCaptureFailed()` — the loop's deliberately no-trap path (`:333-337`) — and
delivers `.captureFailed` once. A `start()` refusal (`.unavailable`/`.alreadyStarted`) throws to
the caller (the machine's chord route surfaces it); nothing is started, no notice is owed.

**D6 — ASR/reply failure is the honest drop (O10).** No injection (the driver names no injector —
its init has none; R2's structural prohibition is the mode machine's, and the driver makes it
vacuously true by construction), no hang, one `.asrFailed`/`.replyFailed` notice, and the loop
left in `.committed` — where the machine is designed to keep listening: the next speech starts a
fresh utterance and **supersedes** the dropped turn (the reply-end race precedent,
`TurnTakingLoop.swift:216-225`). An empty transcript (the seam's legitimate answer,
`ASREngine.swift:29-37`) is a **silent** skip — nothing to reply to, no notice (the dictation
empty-skip policy). Cleanup failure yields raw (the timeout-yields-raw policy, R8 — the driver
enforces the provider's budget with the injected clock, the `cleanIfWired` race's doctrine, since
the pipeline's copy is private and pinned). A render/play failure after `scheduleReply` closes
the window honestly (`reportPlaybackEnded()`, `.playing → .listening`) and delivers
`.replyFailed`. A user stop mid-pipeline cancels the child task: no `scheduleReply`, **no
notice** — the user asked to stop.

**D7 — PROBE-CONVERSE drives the real driver over probe fakes, inside the interposer.** A new
drive `exerciseConverseLoop()` composes `ConverseLoopDriver` with the fallbacks (`EnergyVAD` +
`SilenceThresholdDetector` — the G6/G7 default-work pin), a probe ASR double, a recording probe
cleanup provider, **the shipped minimal reply generator** (reply-seam's deterministic stand-in),
the probe stub synthesizer, a probe fake playback engine and a probe hand-moved clock — no model
artifact, no SDK, no URL. It drives one scripted conversation with a barge-in through the whole
recipe and reports deterministically; the zero-network suite asserts the verbatim post-condition
+ a guard-the-guard, and the placeholder list's `AppBootstrap.self` is replaced by the drive's
minted witness (the `SpeechDrive`/`TurnLoopDrive` precedent — the module entry cannot outlive the
call).

**D8 — The G5 pin re-anchor is a dedicated commit immediately after the wiring commit.**
Reconciling `prd.md:281` ("the pin re-anchor lands in its first commit, before any `AppBootstrap`
edit") with R9's "dedicated, reviewed commit": the re-anchor is decided and its rationale written
**here, before any edit** (the plan + the unit record), the wiring is the **one** AppBootstrap
commit of the aspect (the "first" — no other commit touches the file), and the re-anchor commit
recomputes the digest from the tree and edits only the pin's third row. The wiring commit's red
pin is the pin **working** — its own words ("a deliberate edit fails CI until the digest is
recomputed and edited in review", `TurnTakingComposedAcceptanceTests.swift:341-344`); the two
commits are reviewed as one PR, and CI gates the PR, not the intermediate commit. The first two
digests stay byte-for-byte — the `git diff --stat` discipline proves their files are in no commit.
