### 1.1 Design decisions — the spec's open questions, closed here

- **The real adapter is executed by nothing in CI — decision (b).** The spec left open whether
  to drive `DefaultLLMTransport` headlessly via a `URLProtocol` stub or to leave it to the
  founder's smoke run (mirroring `DefaultModelTransport`). **Closed toward (b):** the adapter
  is translation only (request pass-through, `URLError`→`LLMTransportError` mapping), exactly
  the `DefaultModelTransport` shape, which is likewise executed by nothing in CI
  (`SMOKE_CHECKLIST.md` step 17 is its first execution). A `URLProtocol` stub is new test
  machinery with Swift-6 strict-concurrency risk and no later aspect reuses it; the product's
  honesty is guaranteed by the **decisions above the seam**, which the stub-based tests do
  cover, and by the founder's real Ollama/BYOK smoke runs. The B2 contract therefore reframes
  to: the error **vocabulary** is pinned and the **stub** can emit every failure mode (so
  future providers test their own mapping over it); the real adapter's translation is
  documented as the smoke-verified surface.
- **The transport is body-size-unaware and decision-free.** `URLSession.data(for:)` (JSON
  bodies, no streaming — Ollama/BYOK responses are small, unlike the Parakeet artifact); no
  retry, no timeout of its own (the caller's budget race owns time, `prd.md` M1). Errors carry
  **no body** — a server echo can never leak a key the BYOK provider put in a header.
- **Vocabulary lives beside the adapter** (the `ModelTransport` precedent — the seam lives in
  the module that owns the network family, not in Core).

## 6. Edge Cases & Error Handling

- `URLError.cancelled` → `CancellationError()` (the caller's budget race / Esc depend on it —
  the chain rethrows cancellation, `cleanup-chain` B5).
- Connection-family `URLError` (`.cannotConnectToHost`, `.notConnectedToInternet`,
  `.timedOut`, `.cannotFindHost`, `.networkConnectionLost`) → `.unreachable`.
- Non-2xx → `.serverStatus(code)`; body deliberately not carried (no key/echo leak).
- Anything else (bad URL, transport-level) → `.invalidResponse`.
- The adapter never retries; the caller's race is the only timeout.
