## Phase 1 — the sink on `LatencyLedger`

**Goal:** a finalized record can reach an observer, exactly once, without decorating the ledger.

`LatencyLedger` gains an optional `@Sendable` sink invoked **after** a record is complete and
appended. It must not be able to fail or block the finalize: `finalize` still returns its `Bool` on
its own terms, and the sink's work does not change it.

**RED:** a ledger with a sink installed delivers each finalized record to it once; a ledger without
one behaves exactly as before. Watch it fail against today's ledger (no sink to install — so the
RED is the test asserting delivery counts, written against a sink parameter that must be added;
if that cannot compile, assert behaviourally that two ledgers configured differently are
indistinguishable, the shape used twice already on this branch).

**Files:** `Sources/VoccaCore/LatencyLedger.swift`, `Tests/HarnessTests/LatencyLedgerTests.swift`.

**Watch:** `snapshot()`/`describe()` semantics and the 512 cap must not change. The probe reads
`describe()`.

**Commit:** `usage-wiring: a sink on the ledger, so a finalized record can be observed once`

## Phase 2 — the day provider, and its seam

**Goal:** resolve a wall-clock instant into a **local** `CalendarDay`, in `VoccaUsage`.

⚠️ **Check the lint rules FIRST.** `Calendar`, `TimeZone` and `Date` would be new system-framework
names in `VoccaUsage`. `InjectionSeamBoundaryTests` maintains per-family tables for `FileManager`
and `UserDefaults`; the `latency-instrumentation` PRD records that the one-file-per-seam rule
applies to any new system-framework name. Determine what the rules actually require — a new family
table, a row in an existing one, or nothing — and satisfy it. **Do not weaken a lint to avoid it,
and do not invent a table the rules do not ask for.** Report what you found either way.

**Shape:** a small `CalendarDayProvider` (a `@Sendable` closure or a tiny type) that answers
"today, locally". Injected, never read from `VoccaCore`.

**Tests:** a fixed instant in a fixed time zone yields the expected `CalendarDay`; and the
provider is called **per fold**, not memoised (E3 — an app running past midnight starts a new day).
Prefer injecting the clock and calendar so the test is deterministic rather than dependent on the
machine's zone.

**Commit:** `usage-wiring: resolve today's local calendar day at the adapter seam`

## Phase 3 — fold on finalize, load at launch

**Goal:** the composition holds a live `UsageWindow`, seeded from disk and grown by the sink.

**Files:** `Sources/VoccaBootstrap/AppBootstrap.swift`, plus whatever small holder type the fold
needs (a `UsageRecorder` actor in `VoccaUsage` owning the window, the store and the provider is the
natural shape — it keeps `AppBootstrap` to composition).

- `configure` constructs the store, loads the window once, and installs the ledger sink.
- Each finalized record folds into the day the provider names, in memory.
- A load failure yields an empty window and never blocks a dictation (D8).

**Tests:** D1, D2, D7, D8, D9 from the spec. Drive through the real composition where the harness
can, and through the holder type where it cannot — `AppBootstrap.configure` is `@MainActor`, builds
an audio graph and an event tap, and is executed by nothing in CI, so the source-scan precedent
(`SpeechTabWiringTests`, `SessionKindWiringTests`) applies for the composition-level facts.

**Commit:** `usage-wiring: fold every finalized session into the day it happened on`

## Phase 4 — write cadence, off the dictation path

**Goal:** the window reaches disk without a write ever happening inside a dictation.

- **Debounced**: at most one write per interval. Pick the interval, pin it in one named constant
  with its reasoning (it bounds how many counts a crash can lose — and losing counts is acceptable
  where losing a transcript never is).
- **On day rollover**: the previous day is complete.
- **On termination**: wired in `AppBootstrap`. `App/VoccaApp.swift` is pinned to an exact shim by
  `BundleConfigurationTests.testAppTargetSourceIsOnlyAShimToTheBootstrapModule` and cannot hold
  lifecycle code.

**D3 is the criterion that matters**: assert on the injected filesystem seam's call log that a
fold alone performs **no** write. Not by inspection — on the recorded calls.

**Commit:** `usage-wiring: write on rollover, on termination, and otherwise debounced`

## Edge cases

| Case | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Load fails at launch | Empty window; dictation unaffected; one loud log |
| Midnight passes with the app running | Next fold lands on the new day (provider called per fold) |
| Termination during a debounce window | The write happens on termination |
| Crash during a debounce window | Counts since the last write are lost, bounded by the interval. No transcript is involved |
| A fold for a day already evicted by retention | Retention applies on insertion; the window stays bounded |
