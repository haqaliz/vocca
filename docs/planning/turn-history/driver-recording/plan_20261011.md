## Decisions made in planning

- **D1 (seam):** `VoccaCore/Conversation/ConversationRecording.swift` (shipped by aspect 1;
  Foundation-free, `async`, never `async throws` — the `local-data-provider` lesson). The driver
  holds only the protocol.
- **D2 (record point, recorded deviation):** the PRD words it as "the `.speakReply` effect";
  implemented at the commit point in `runUtterancePipeline` (after the cleaned `raw` and the
  computed `reply`, before `loop.scheduleReply(reply)`) — the one place both texts exist. The
  awaited call strictly precedes playback scheduling, which is what makes D3's attribution
  provable.
- **D3 (interrupt):** at the `.bargeIn` application, after `converseReplySink(nil)`:
  `Task { await recorder.markLastTurnInterrupted() }`. Safe ordering by D2; the store owns the
  empty/no-turn no-op.
- **D4 (additivity):** `conversationRecorder: (any ConversationRecording)? = nil` (after
  `converseReplySink`); `composeConverseWiring` gains the same defaulted parameter and passes it
  through. Probes compose by default → byte-identical.
- **D5 (spy):** `ConversationRecorderSpy` (an actor recording `(utterance, reply)` pairs and
  mark counts, order-stamped) in the new test file; the scripted harness is the
  `TurnTakingComposedAcceptanceTests` one.

## Testing strategy

All scripted/offline over the injected clock; the spy proves calls and order; no real audio, no
store, no file. Counterfactuals recorded for first-pass-green tests.

## Environment & privacy

No env vars, no files. The recorder is `nil` everywhere except the composed app (aspect 3); a
`nil` recorder means the capability does not exist at runtime.

## Edge cases

Barge-in before any turn; barge-in spanning turns (two rapid barges → two marks, the store
no-ops the second by position); turn with an empty cleaned transcript (text is whatever the
pipeline produced — no special case); reply identical to utterance (the echo default) still
records; the driver restarted mid-session (records continue through the same recorder).
