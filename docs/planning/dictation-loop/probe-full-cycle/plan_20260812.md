## Testing strategy

- The interposer suite is the test: zero `connect(2)` across session + ladder + full
  cycle; positive and negative controls intact.
- No real engine, no mic, no window server in the probe (stub engine + fake graph +
  probe ladder fakes only).

## Environment & privacy

- No env vars. The probe IS the privacy invariant's enforcement point.

## Edge cases & error handling

- The root's tap returns `.unavailable` under the probe (no Accessibility grant) — the
  drive must not depend on the tap; it drives the sink directly (existing pattern).
- `AppBootstrap.configure` is called by the probe already (`VoccaNetworkProbe.swift:211-215`)
  — the full-cycle drive uses the same composed objects; do not construct a second root.
- If the root's background engine prepare would download, the probe must never trigger
  a download: the probe drives the pipeline with the stub engine, not the real one —
  assert no download session starts during the probe run.
